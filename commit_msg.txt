fix: resolve primary button contrast accessibility warning

The primary indigo color (#6366f1) used for active toggle buttons, "Next" buttons, and the skip-to-main-content link had a contrast ratio of exactly 4.49:1 against white text. The WCAG AA standard requires 4.5:1, meaning it was failing by a microscopic 0.01 margin.

Changed --primary-color and globals.css references to a slightly darker indigo (#4f46e5), boosting the contrast ratio to a perfectly accessible 6.4:1.
