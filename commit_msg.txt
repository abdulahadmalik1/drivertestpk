fix: remove backdrop-filter and fadeIn animation causing NO_FCP

Two root causes of NO_FCP on PageSpeed Insights identified and fixed:

1. backdrop-filter: blur(20px) on .glass-card requires GPU compositing.
   Lighthouse uses headless Chrome in a virtual environment with no GPU.
   This caused the entire paint pipeline to stall, resulting in NO_FCP.
   Fixed: replaced with solid rgba(15,23,42,0.92) background.

2. animation: fadeIn 0.4s starts .glass-card at opacity:0.
   Elements with opacity:0 are NOT counted as "painted" by Lighthouse.
   ALL page content is inside .glass-card so nothing was ever "visible".
   Fixed: removed the animation entirely.

These two issues existed in the original codebase and caused every
single Lighthouse metric to show Error! even before our other changes.
